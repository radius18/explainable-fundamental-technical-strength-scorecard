# Portfolio Notes

This repository presents a concise public case study of an explainable Thinkorswim/ThinkScript scorecard and the QA work used to identify and correct a missing-data scoring defect.

The public portfolio focuses on design, scoring logic, QA/UAT, data validation, debugging, explainability, and selected evidence. It intentionally avoids claims of predictive accuracy, trading performance, or broad cross-instrument validation.
